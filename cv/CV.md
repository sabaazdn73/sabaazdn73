## Projects

### Tnega: what a tokenized stock really costs to buy
*Founder · 2026 · [tnega.app](https://www.tnega.app)*

- tnega.app shows what it costs to buy a tokenized stock or ETF on each chain, fees included. When something can't be measured, it says so instead of showing a made-up number.
- It has a free MCP server (mcp.tnega.app) with no account or key. An AI assistant can use it to compare costs, check a wallet's holdings, and prepare a buy or sell. You sign in your own wallet, and Tnega never holds your money or keys.
- I wrote an escrow contract and deployed it on four chains: BNB Chain, Ethereum, Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain. It passed 33 hostile test cases on a copy of the real network, and a code scan found nothing serious.
- I also measure how markets behave. For example, one trader address had every one of its 153,727 post-only orders refused in a single market, while the typical trader sees about 0.15%.

### F2F Cross-Border: sending money across borders without wallets
*Led the design · UZH Summer School and ETHGlobal Lisbon · Jul 2026 · [f2f-uzh.vercel.app](https://f2f-uzh.vercel.app)*
